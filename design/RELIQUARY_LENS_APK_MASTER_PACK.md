## A. What this game is

It is her birthday. Gokul hands Aishwarya a flat printed ring card with their wedding ring on it, and the Samsung Galaxy Tab S7 / S7+ (landscape) with the Lens open.

She points the Lens at the card. Gold light traces the real ring, closes into a circle, and grows into a large **AR gold ring** in the air.

The screen fogs. She blows. Fog clears. Behind it, **Aishwarya** appears in gold. No welcome page.

She places the AR gold ring on a surface. It stays pinned with a heartbeat glow. Out of it rises a **memory snowglobe** (photos/clips in a circle). Later, when media is ready: **Friends montage**, then **Uncle ghost montage** as separate flows (not mixed into snowglobe until attached).

The hunt begins. Gold-ink riddles, wax seals on scanned hunt cards, letter collection (Capsule Click). After each chapter’s cards she solves the vault word at the AR gold ring (music-box dials). Vaults: **MICROWAVECUPBOARD** · **GOLDJEWELRYPOUCH** · **UNDERSTAIRS**.

Card **23** is only a hunt card hidden behind her late uncle’s framed photo — different image; **no** uncle walk-out from that scan. After card 23, she aims at the **pooja tall boy**; a discreet AR marker on the tall boy triggers mist and a ghostly full-body uncle walk-out (Google Flow video). Captions: mixed Tamil + English.

Cards **28–29** are at the Toyota RAV4 2024. Card **29** hide spot is the **car boot** (not the key chain).

After vault UNDERSTAIRS she opens the real gift, taps **Finish Quest**, sees a letter that one more grand gift awaits, rescans the **physical wedding ring**, the ring rises, caption **Look up** — Gokul is there. No Protocol 0510 typing. No candle. No eclipse.

About 75–120 minutes. Offline. Tablet charged.

## D. Locked design moments

### Opening
- Flat printed ring card + real wedding ring → gold trace → AR gold ring → fog → blow → **Aishwarya**.
- No welcome page.

### AR gold ring home
- Place on any flat surface; drag / rotate / resize; stays pinned; heartbeat glow.

### Hunt
- Gold wax seal on scan; riddle writes in gold ink; shimmer-tune lock-on; Capsule Click letters.

### Vaults
- Music-box dials at the AR gold ring.
- Sounds: Latch Click / Velvet Pocket / Stairs Dust Drift.

### Uncle
- **Not** card 23. **Not** scanning the uncle framed photo.
- Player: aim at **pooja tall boy**. Tech: high-contrast print/marker on tall boy (bare wood unreliable for ARCore).
- Mist → ghostly full-body; Google Flow; captions mixed Tamil + English.

### Montage
- Snowglobe after ring place.
- Friends montage + Uncle ghost montage = separate later slots after snowglobe.

### Ending
- Finish Quest → letter (one more grand gift) → rescan **physical wedding ring** → rise → **Look up** → Gokul.
- No 0510 typing. No candle. No eclipse. No gift rising from AR home ring as the final gift.

### Mini-Me
- Named **"Gokul"**. 2D caption edge. 2–3 expressions. Captions only — no voice.

### UI
- Clean camera; menu + help; light blurred caption strip.
- Living Glass: navy / gold / cyan. Fonts: Cinzel / Source Sans 3 / Caveat.
- **No rehearsal PIN** — Gokul around her for testing/help.

### Sensors
- Mic (blow), tilt (dials), light (Night Lens), vibration. Compass optional for uncle direction.

### Car
- Toyota RAV4 2024. Card 29 = **boot**.

### Engine
- Unity 6 URP + AR Foundation + ARCore. Repo: `gokulkrish140795-droid/project-ar-app`. Offline landscape.
